First, start with the basics. Understand the road is a shared space and bicyclists should follow the<br>same rules and responsibilities as drivers:<br>Ride in a straight line, single file, in the same direction as traffic.<br>Obey all traffic signs and signals.<br>Stop and look left-right-left for traffic before entering a street.<br>Bikers should use bike lanes whenever available.<br>Bikers should always wear a helmet that fits level on the head and low on<br>the forehead — one or two finger-widths above your eyebrow. Also, be sure<br>to adjust the slider on both side straps to form a “V” shape under, and<br>slightly in front of, the ears. Lock the slider if possible.
